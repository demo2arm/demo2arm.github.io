demo2arm

differentiable co-design from demonstrations to task-specific robotic arms

paper video code

overview

demo2arm is a differentiable co-design framework that transforms demonstration trajectories and interaction wrenches into task-specific robotic arm designs.

method

the framework jointly optimizes robot kinematics, dynamics, and structural geometry using differentiable rigid-body dynamics and finite element analysis.

results

demo2arm generates robotic arm designs adapted to the motion and loading requirements of demonstrated tasks.
